Frequently Asked Questions

What shipping data do I need to provide for the audit?

You'll need a CSV or PDF export of your shipping invoices from your UPS or FedEx account portal. Most clients have this in 10 minutes. We walk you through exactly where to find it if needed. No other systems access or account credentials are required.

Who actually reviews my shipping data?

Our founder reviews every account personally. There is no junior analyst layer. Chris's 15+ years of carrier-side experience means he can identify the specific line items with room to move, not just flag anything outside an industry average.

What is a performance-based or contingency model, exactly?

A performance-based model means we invoice based on a percentage of the savings we recover, billed weekly. Billing cadence is negotiable and can be discussed during the audit conversation. If we don't save you money, there is no invoice. There's no retainer, no upfront fee, and no fixed monthly charge.

What happens if Smart LGSTX can't find meaningful savings?

We'll tell you in the estimate call, before you submit any data. We don't start an audit on accounts where we don't believe meaningful savings exist, because we only earn when you save.

How does the carrier transition work if I have to switch?

Smart LGSTX manages the carrier transition completely. We handle the account setup, rate negotiation, and timeline coordination so your fulfillment operations aren't disrupted. Most clients continue shipping without any gap in service.

Can I get an estimate without submitting my shipping data?

Yes. The first step is a free 15-minute estimate call based on your product category, annual shipping volume, and carrier. You get a specific dollar range for potential savings before you share any data.

Is my shipping data kept confidential?

Yes. Your shipping data is used solely to identify savings opportunities in your carrier contract. It is not shared with carriers, third parties, or used for any other purpose.

Does Smart LGSTX handle international shipping contracts?

Yes, international is included in the audit at no additional cost. Carrier contracts have separate international rate structures, customs-related fees, and fuel surcharge tables negotiated independently from domestic terms. Our founder's carrier-side background includes international accounts. If you have international volume, we audit it alongside your domestic data and identify savings on both.
