Your Privacy Choices

You decide what this browser shares with Meta for ads. One switch, no form, no account.

We also honor Global Privacy Control automatically.

Facebook and Instagram ads

Share my activity with Meta

You have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. When this is on, the Meta Pixel tells Meta which pages you visit here and what you do, like booking a call. We use that to measure our ads and show them to people who have visited, and under California law that may count as a "sale" or "sharing". Turn it off to opt out.

Opt-Out Request Honored

Sharing is on in this browser.

  • Saved in this browserUsing another browser or device? Set it there too. Clearing your cookies resets it.

What stays on

Google Analytics, which counts visits so we can improve the site, keeps running. So do our forms, the savings calculator and booking a call.

Sensitive personal information

California also lets you limit how a business uses sensitive personal information, like government ID numbers or precise location. We don't ask for that kind of information on this site, so there is nothing to limit.
