The Person Negotiating For You Used to Be the Person Negotiating Against You.

50+ years combined on the carrier side. Now working from the other side of the table.

Chris Tweedy, Founder & CEO

Chris Tweedy spent 15+ years at one of the nation's largest carriers in sales and customer solutions. During that time, he sat across the table from thousands of shipping negotiations, on the carrier's side.

He knew which line items companies fought for. He knew which concessions the carrier was willing to make, and which ones they'd never offer unless pushed. He knew which surcharge categories had real flexibility, and which carrier reps were bluffing.

He also knew that most businesses, even large, sophisticated ones, left significant money on the table in every negotiation. Not because they weren't smart. Because they didn't have someone who'd seen both sides.

After leaving the carrier, Chris spent time as a shipping executive at a growth-stage company. Sitting on the buyer's side for the first time, he watched his own carrier rep use every tactic he'd once relied on.

He launched Smart LGSTX with a simple thesis: companies deserve an expert who knows how carriers actually think.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the founder's background in carrier negotiations?

Our founder spent 15+ years at one of the major carriers in sales and customer solutions, conducting carrier-side negotiations with thousands of businesses. He knew which concessions the carrier was willing to make, which surcharge categories had flexibility, and which tactics carrier reps used to protect margins.

Why was Smart LGSTX started?

After 15+ years on the carrier side, our founder moved to the buyer's side as a shipping executive at a growth-stage company. Sitting in that seat for the first time, he watched his own carrier rep use every negotiation tactic he'd once relied on, and saw first-hand how much money was being left on the table.

Does Smart LGSTX work with specific industries or any shipper?

Smart LGSTX focuses on companies shipping heavy, bulky, or oversized products where carrier surcharges stack up significantly on every shipment: furniture, mattress and bedding, auto parts, building materials, fitness equipment, food and beverage, and manufacturing.

How established is Smart LGSTX as a business?

Smart LGSTX recovered over $2.5 million in shipping savings for clients in its second year of operation. The business was built almost entirely on referrals: no cold outreach, no advertising. The client base spans multiple verticals.

Does the founder still personally handle every negotiation?

Yes. Every shipping audit is reviewed personally and every carrier negotiation is led directly. Smart LGSTX is built around specific insider expertise. There is no junior team layer between leadership and your account.

Does Smart LGSTX cover international shipping, both imports and exports?

Yes. International shipping is covered, both imports and exports. Chris's time on the carrier side included work on international accounts, which means he understands the surcharge structures, customs fee schedules, and zone-based pricing that major carriers apply to cross-border shipments. If you have international volume, it's included in your audit.
